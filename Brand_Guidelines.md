# THE INK MOON — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

### Mission
To create and celebrate original, culturally rooted art that transforms spaces into narratives. Every piece is hand-crafted by a single artist, ensuring authenticity and intellectual depth.

### Vision
The Ink Moon is the trusted partner for brands, hospitality, and collectors seeking art that doesn't just fill walls—it creates conversations and embodies a commitment to originality and heritage.

### Core Values
- **Authenticity:** 100% original, hand-made work; no production lines
- **Heritage:** Rooted in Indian cultural narratives and design traditions
- **Craftsmanship:** Intellectual rigor meets artistic excellence
- **Collaboration:** Customization and bespoke creation for unique brand stories
- **Quality:** Premium materials, finishing, and attention to detail

---

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Characteristics
- Premium but accessible
- Thoughtful and educational
- Art-focused and culturally aware
- Professional without being corporate
- Authentic and personal

### Tone Examples

**Warm & Collaborative:**
"Let's create something that tells your story—we work with you to shape the vision into reality."

**Confident & Knowledgeable:**
"Every piece is conceived and executed by hand. No shortcuts, no outsourcing—just authentic creation."

**Heritage-Forward:**
"Rooted in Indian heritage, our work explores the intersection of cultural narrative and spatial experience."

### What NOT to Do
- Don't sound generic or templated
- Don't oversell or use hype language
- Don't downplay the hand-made, artisanal nature
- Don't treat art as decoration; frame it as transformation

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

**Primary:**
- **Gold/Brass:** `#C9A961` 
  - Usage: Brand logo, headlines, accent elements
  - Meaning: Luxury, warmth, heritage
  - Emotional: Timeless, premium

**Secondary:**
- **Cream/Off-White:** `#F5F1E8`
  - Usage: Background, breathing room, premium feel
  - Meaning: Elegance, simplicity
  - Emotional: Clean, curated

**Neutral:**
- **Dark Charcoal:** `#2B2B2B`
  - Usage: Body text, depth, contrast
  - Meaning: Sophistication, clarity
  - Emotional: Professional, grounded

**Accent:**
- **Soft Sage:** `#B8C5A6`
  - Usage: Optional accent in specific artwork/design
  - Meaning: Natural, balanced
  - Emotional: Calming, organic

### Typography

**Headlines/Titles:**
- Serif font (elegant, sophisticated)
- Generous tracking
- Bold or semi-bold weights
- All-caps only for very specific headers

**Body Copy:**
- Serif font (maintains premium feel)
- 16px+ for web, 11pt+ for print
- Line height: 1.6+ for readability
- Rich blacks (#2B2B2B), not pure black

**Digital:**
- Max 60-75 characters per line
- Generous margins and whitespace

**Print:**
- Art book aesthetic
- Dense/quiet alternating spreads
- Plenty of whitespace around imagery

### Photography & Imagery

**Artwork:**
- Showcase full pieces with proper lighting
- Show framing context when relevant
- Use neutral backgrounds (light cream, soft grey)
- Never crop or compress artwork

**Lifestyle/Environment:**
- Warm, natural lighting
- Curated, minimal interiors
- People optional (if included, real, not stock)
- Emphasize how art transforms space

**Don't:**
- Use stock photography
- Oversaturate or over-filter
- Compete with artwork for attention
- Use trendy/dated visual effects

### Layout & Composition

**Spacing:**
- 80/20 rule: 80% whitespace/breathing room, 20% content
- Generous margins (minimum 40px web, 1" print)
- Breathing room around headlines

**Grid System:**
- Clean, hierarchical
- Asymmetrical layouts are OK (but intentional)
- Artwork should be hero element, not background

**Framing:**
- Minimal borders/frames
- When needed, use real wood or subtle borders matching brand colors
- Never busy or ornate

## Brand Applications

### Website
- Premium, minimal aesthetic
- Artwork should be prominently featured
- Easy navigation to collections, commissions, B2B info
- Contact & inquiry should be clear & prominent

### Proposals & Pitches
- Professional presentation deck format
- Include sample artwork prominently
- Clear pricing & timeline
- Personalized to client (not templated)

### Catalogues
- Art book quality
- Dense/quiet alternating spreads
- High-quality imagery of all pieces
- Printed on premium paper

### Email & Digital Communication
- Warm, personal greeting
- Professional but not robotic
- Always include relevant artwork/imagery
- Clear CTA (call-to-action)

### Social Media
- High-quality imagery of artwork
- Behind-the-scenes process content
- Artist perspective/narrative
- Engagement-friendly captions (warm, conversational)

## In Practice

**When creating anything for The Ink Moon, ask:**
- Does this celebrate the hand-made, artisanal nature?
- Does this honor the artist's voice and heritage focus?
- Does this feel premium without being stuffy?
- Would a collector feel this is authentic?
- Does this make art the hero, not background?

If yes to all: You're on brand. 🌙
