# THE INK MOON — Brand Kit

**Premium original wall art and design rooted in Indian heritage**

## Brand Overview

The Ink Moon is a premium art studio creating bespoke wall art, curated collections, and customized corporate gifting. Every piece is hand-created by artist and founder Anoushka—no production lines, no outsourcing.

**Key Differentiators:**
- 100% original artwork by single artist
- Customization expertise (color palettes, text integration, compositional edits)
- Comprehensive B2B offerings for hotels, corporates, and retail
- Tailored corporate gifting programs
- Flexible commission and bulk pricing structures

**Founded:** By Anoushka (Artist & Founder)  
**Location:** Greater Noida, UP  
**Background:** Psychology degree (University of Bristol), RCA London training

## Brand Identity

### Color Palette
- **Gold/Brass:** `#C9A961` — Primary brand color, luxury, warmth
- **Cream/Off-White:** `#F5F1E8` — Background, premium feel
- **Dark Charcoal:** `#2B2B2B` — Text, depth
- **Soft Sage:** `#B8C5A6` — Accent (used in some artwork)

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Serif (elegant, sophisticated)
- **Body Copy:** Serif with good readability
- **Brand Voice:** Premium, professional, authentic, art-focused
- **Tone:** Educational but not clinical; warm but not casual

### Visual Style
- Clean, minimal layouts
- Emphasis on artwork & whitespace
- Premium paper/material textures
- Art book aesthetic in catalogues
- Dense/quiet alternating spreads

## Materials & Customization

### Print Materials
**Paper Option:**
- 170 GSM fine art cotton paper
- Matte finish, archival quality
- Requires glass framing

**Canvas Option:**
- 280 GSM poly-cotton canvas
- Gallery-quality print finish
- Does NOT require glass framing

### Framing & Finishing Options

**Paper:**
- Rolled/Frameless
- Wood Look Frame with Glass
- Real Wood Frame with Glass

**Canvas:**
- Rolled/Frameless
- Gallery Wrap (stretched canvas, frameless)
- Wood Look Frame
- Real Wood Frame

**Frame Colors:**

## Terms & Conditions

1. **IP Rights:** All artwork produced under commission remains IP of The Ink Moon. Reproduction rights not transferred unless explicitly agreed in writing.

2. **Engagement Model:** The Ink Moon is engaged as a creative production partner, not as a vendor assigning ownership of artwork.

3. **Licensing:** Commissioned prints licensed for buyer's internal use or resale only. Use in advertising, brand campaigns, or derivative works requires prior written approval and may attract additional licensing fees.

4. **Portfolio Display:** The Ink Moon retains the right to display all commissioned work in portfolio unless confidentiality clause separately agreed.

5. **Order Confirmation:** All orders subject to signed purchase order or written confirmation before production begins.

6. **Price Changes:** Confirmed orders invoiced at rate agreed at time of order.

7. **Edits & Revisions:** Two free edits per piece included; additional revisions charged separately.
