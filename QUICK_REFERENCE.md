## 📊 Key Information

### Materials
**Paper:** 170 GSM fine art cotton (requires framing)  
**Canvas:** 280 GSM poly-cotton (no framing needed)

### Standard Sizes
**Square:** 10×10, 12×12, 16×16, 20×20, 24×24, 30×30, 36×36  
**Rectangle:** 9×12, 12×16, 15×20, 18×24, 24×32, 21×36
